◈ How Community Hubs Work

Own a piece of SocialHub. Launch a themed Hub, attract creators, and earn from every interaction inside your community.

1
Start a Community Hub

Launch your own themed Hub for a one-time cost of 1,000 ★. Choose a name, write a description, and provide a header image URL. Your Hub is listed in the global Communities directory the moment it goes live.

2
Broadcasting Inside a Hub

Broadcast costs inside a Hub are identical to the global feed: Text 1.2 ★ Image 3 ★ Video 10 ★. 100% of the broadcast fee goes to the platform. Posts made inside a Hub appear only in that Hub’s feed — they do not appear in the Global Feed.

3
Owner Hosting — 80% on Your Own Posts

When you post inside your own Hub and someone likes or comments, you earn 80% (0.8 ★) directly into your earned-star balance. The platform keeps 20% (0.2 ★).

4
Landlord Split — 40% on Guest Posts & Comments

When a guest posts in your Hub and receives a like or a comment, the split is three-way: the guest creator earns 40% (0.4 ★), you (the Hub owner) earn 40% (0.4 ★) as Landlord Earnings, and the platform keeps 20% (0.2 ★). Every landlord earn event is labelled “Landlord Earnings from [Hub Name]” in your Ledger.

5
Comment Hypes Inside a Hub — 40 / 40 / 20

When User B likes User A’s comment inside your Hub, the split is: 40% (0.4 ★) to User A (the commenter), 40% (0.4 ★) to you as Landlord Earnings, and 20% (0.2 ★) to the platform.

6
Community Memberships & the Unified Following Feed

Inside any Hub, tap + Follow Hub to become a member. Following a Community costs 1 ★, with 90% (0.9 ★) going directly to the Community Creator’s earned-star balance and 10% (0.1 ★) to the platform.

Once you follow a Hub, its posts appear automatically in the ♡ Following tab on the home feed alongside posts from individual creators you follow — giving you a single, unified curated stream.

◈ How SocialHub Works

A decentralised social platform where every interaction has real value. Six things to know before you broadcast.

1
Connect & Set Your Identity

Click Connect Wallet in the top-right to link your Web3 wallet. Open the Profile tab, enter a display name and paste an avatar image URL, then save your identity. Your profile is tied to your wallet address and is visible across all devices automatically.

2
Stars ★ — Your On-Platform Currency

Every action on SocialHub costs Stars ★. 1 Star = $0.01 USD, always pegged to the live $APTM token price shown in the top bar. Deposit $APTM via the Wallet panel to receive Stars instantly. Stars are your spending power — use them to post, like, comment, and boost.

3
Broadcast & Signal Costs

Every broadcast deducts Stars from your balance:
Text Post 1.2 ★ Image 3 ★ Video 10 ★ Boost to Top 10 ★

Media files are stored securely in the cloud — no wallet popup required for uploads.

Daily Signals: Feature any post at the top of the feed for 24 hours for just 1 ★. Any user can promote any post.

4
The 80 / 20 Creator Split — Global Feed

Liking a post or leaving a comment costs 1 Star. On the Global Feed, the creator or commenter always receives a strict 80% (0.8 ★) directly into their earned-star balance. The platform keeps 20% (0.2 ★). All interactions are completely gas-free — no wallet popup, ever.

Inside a Community Hub, a different split applies — tap the ℹ info button on any Hub for the full breakdown.

5
Direct Support & Following — 90 / 10 Split

On any creator's Profile Wall, you can support them directly with two actions:

❤ Like — Costs 1 ★. The creator receives 90% (0.9 ★) into their earned-star balance instantly. The platform keeps 10% (0.1 ★).

★ Tip — Choose a preset (1, 5, 10 ★), roll a random amount with the 🎲 dice, or enter any custom amount. The same 90 / 10 split applies to every tip. Small tips (1–5 ★) trigger a sparkle shower animation; larger tips (10 ★+) trigger a full high-impact star shower across the screen.

+ Follow — Costs 1 ★ as bot-prevention. The creator receives 0.9 ★. Once you follow someone, their posts appear in your ♥ Following tab on the home feed — your personal curated stream. Every Like, Tip, and Follow is recorded in both parties' Creator Studio Ledgers.

6
Settle — Withdraw Your Stars to Your Wallet

Open the Creator Studio tab and click Settle Total Balance to Wallet. Your full balance — purchased Stars plus earned Stars — is converted back to $APTM at the live price and sent directly to your wallet. No lock-up period. Withdraw any time.
